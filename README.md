# 🥦 GreenGrocer Business Analytics Project

## 📊 Overview

This project is part of a **Module 12 Assignment: Business Analytics Fundamentals and Applications**. It simulates a real-world grocery business (**GreenGrocer**) and applies data analytics techniques to generate insights, visualize trends, and make business recommendations.

The project covers the full analytics pipeline:

* Descriptive Analytics
* Diagnostic Analytics
* Predictive Analytics
* Business Insights & Recommendations

---

## 🗂️ Project Structure

The script performs the following major tasks:

### 1. Data Generation

Synthetic datasets are created for:

* **Sales Data** (daily transactions across stores and departments)
* **Customer Data** (demographics, segments, spending behavior)
* **Store Data** (size, staff, marketing spend)
* **Operational Metrics** (sales efficiency, profit, satisfaction)

---

### 2. Descriptive Analytics

* Total sales and profit calculations
* Average profit margins
* Sales breakdown by:

  * Store
  * Department
* Visualizations of:

  * Sales distribution
  * Sales over time
* Customer segmentation analysis

---

### 3. Diagnostic Analytics

* Correlation analysis between store features and sales
* Store performance comparisons
* Seasonal trend analysis (monthly & day-of-week patterns)

---

### 4. Predictive Analytics

* Linear regression model to predict store sales
* Department-level sales trend forecasting
* Model evaluation using **R² (coefficient of determination)**

---

### 5. Business Insights

* Identification of:

  * Top-performing store-department combinations
  * Underperforming areas
* Opportunity scoring by store
* Strategic recommendations

---

### 6. Executive Summary

The program outputs a final report including:

* Overview of business performance
* Key findings
* Actionable recommendations
* Expected business impact

---
